This workflow documents the installation of a thermostat or other rooftop unit
(RTU) controls to show a quality installation. It includes installation of a
programmable or networked thermostat (also referred to as smart, connected, or
internet-enabled thermostats), new RTU controller, variable-speed drive for
fans, and the damper actuators and sensors for economizer control and
demand-controlled ventilation (DCV).

        <Collapsible header="Preparing a site for thermostat or RTU control installation">
            - Consider visiting the site ahead of ordering materials so that an appropriate solution can be provided.
                - Most networked thermostats are wireless. Depending on the specific product and scale of the installation, repeaters may be required to boost signal strength to the gateway.
                - If the customer is interested in an ARC Upgrade, look at the existing RTUs to determine what equipment will be needed for the installation. Existing RTUs may or may not have motorized dampers, for example, which are necessary for economizing.
            - If the thermostat or other RTU controls require a gateway for wireless communication, discuss install location with the customer. The gateway will require an internet connection. Discuss the customers' plan for an internet connection and document the Wi-Fi password for the installation. Customers may decide to set up an isolated network for internet-enabled devices or to connect the devices to an existing network. Some manufacturers also offer a cellular option that avoids connecting to a local internet network.
            - Verify you have received all the correct equipment and any problems with the existing equipment are fixed prior to scheduling the installation.
        </Collapsible>

            ## Installation Tips
                - Thermostats should be installed on an interior wall away from direct sun exposure and airflow.
                - Wireless thermostats and gateways should be installed away from metal objects for the best possible communication strength.
                - You will need Wi-Fi credentials to connect the thermostat to the internet.

            ## Install the Gateway
            Install the gateway inside the building. Where possible, avoid installing the gateway in rooms with concrete walls or near other networking equipment to avoid potential interference and improve communication strength. A gateway may not be required for all installations.

Economizing should be enabled when the outdoor air temperature is less than 68F.
